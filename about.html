About

Infrastructure is worth looking at on purpose.

ESUNOS is a small network of newsletters about the built world — the substations, spans and server halls we rely on and refuse to notice — and the culture, history and money behind them.

ESUNOS started as a reaction to the recent global tensions that have been affecting all of our lives. In a world full of divisions, it can be hard to find communities and areas that unite us as humanity. The ESUNOS guide to infrastructure aims to use architectural marvels to create bonds between places and people that no one could ever suspect.

To achieve this we go the extra mile. We aim to provide knowledge that can only be found through dedication, and hopefully on-the-ground research in the future. That's not even the best part... we offer our newsletters for free!

What we aim to cover

Power and the grid. Transport systems and how they work. Housing and apartments with the views of their residents. Wherever infrastructure has shaped lives - that's our scent, which we follow once a fortnight.

One masthead, many guides

ESUNOS is built to be a network. We aim to grow and document many connecting factors of the modern day world. Infrastructure is just our launch pad.
